Non-goals

This release does not provide:

* autonomous execution;
* network propagation;
* enforcement authority;
* certification;
* universal safety claims;
* a universal numerical AI capability threshold.

Revision rule

Future versions should preserve the historical record of:

* additions;
* removals;
* modifications;
* failed hypotheses;
* successful attacks;
* unresolved limitations.

A revision should not silently erase previous epistemic states.
